Where our event information, images and tools come from.

Event information and photographs

Brisbane City Council. Events. Council Open Data.

Titles, descriptions, dates, costs, age information, venues, booking details and event image links come from this dataset. Each activity links to its original Council listing. Photographs may belong to the organiser or another credited creator; follow the original listing for its image credits.

The Council describes this dataset as a daily extract from Trumba, limited to the next 2,000 events. Family Finds loads a small batch at a time, so the listings are not a complete directory of Brisbane activities.

Google sign-in

Google. Sign in with Google for Web — Google Identity Services.

The login and registration pages use Google's official sign-in button and library. The backend verifies the identity token before opening a Family Finds session.

Ask AI event finder

Cloudflare Workers AI and Meta. Llama 3.3 70B Instruct FP8 Fast.

The existing event finder can use this model to turn a message into search filters. A built-in keyword matcher runs when the service is unavailable or the application's daily limit is reached. Suggestions use Council listings; age, pet and access requirements should be checked with the organiser.
